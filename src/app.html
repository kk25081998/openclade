Cite as: Khosa, K. (2026). OpenClade v0.1.1. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23634128
OpenClade 0.1.1 (prototype). Scoring follows MorphyLib/TreeSearch (M. R. Smith; M. D. Brazeau) and Brazeau, Guillerme & Smith (2019) Systematic Biology 68:619–631. Search: random addition sequence, SPR and TBR branch swapping, parsimony ratchet (Nixon 1999), tree fusing (Goloboff 1999), run in parallel across CPU cores.
Example matrices are published datasets distributed with the TreeSearch R package; please cite the original authors.
